Indian Knowledge Systems

This project applies ideas associated with traditional Indian learning practices: niyama (discipline and regularity), reflective self-assessment, teacher-guided learning, and balance between study, rest and daily routine. These ideas are translated into measurable study-habit inputs rather than presented as medical or scientific claims.
